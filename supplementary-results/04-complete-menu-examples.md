# S4. Complete menu examples

[Supplementary results](README.md)

Tables [S4](#table-s4)–[S9](#table-s9) list one menu per profile and objective set under the original Ideal rule. The NSGA-II representative runs and menu choices follow Section 2.4.5. All foods and portions within a meal remain as observed, and an empty entry denotes an observed absent position. The outcome rows describe the exact displayed menus: attainment A (%), attained criteria, carbon C (kg CO$_2$e/day), water W (L/day) and departure D (%). None of these 24 examples attains all nutrient criteria. They illustrate individual choices, while the repeated-run summaries in the main text describe their variability.
