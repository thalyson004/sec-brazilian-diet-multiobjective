# S3. Additional food-quantity comparisons

[Supplementary results](README.md)

Table [S3](#table-s3) extends the main largest-change analysis to two staple codes, beans and white rice, across every profile and algorithm. It asks whether the effect of explicitly including departure is consistent for these foods. For NSGA-II, beans changed from $133.0\pm132.2$ to $105.0\pm119.1$ g/day between NC and ND in F30, but from $219.5\pm197.9$ to $273.0\pm147.0$ in M30. Thus a lower departure objective need not lower every food amount, and the direction is profile-dependent. The means and sample standard deviations use independent compromise menus, with zero for absence; they are descriptive rather than food-wise significance tests.
