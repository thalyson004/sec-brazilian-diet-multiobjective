# 2026-10-01: Confirmatory design frozen

Status: registered before optimization runs. Study ID: `sec2026-pof-person-day-v1`.

The source validation and cohort validation justify 24 selected adult respondent-days. They also expose an environmental coverage boundary: the OSF workbook assigns unpublished zero impacts to public tap water, and other zero-water rows and the unmatched `7800301#1` key lack a defensible positive coefficient. We hold observed public tap water fixed, retain its reported nutrient amounts, and exclude days with any other optimized line lacking strictly positive carbon and water coefficients. The six-per-stratum deterministic selection limits the comparison to a balanced algorithm benchmark. The selection is not a population estimate.

The POF consumption file includes line-level energy, fiber and sodium quantities. Their per-100-g ratios are consistent within food/preparation keys in this release, so the study derives local per-gram coefficients from these recorded quantities. This does not establish independent validity of the original composition source and does not authorize redistribution of TBCA-derived values. If independent composition validation changes the input interpretation, it will require a separate registered sensitivity analysis or a new confirmatory study ID.

The full formulation, ten objective combinations, four algorithms, evaluation limit, planned outputs and statistical contrasts are in [the frozen design](../protocol/README.md) and its machine-readable [configuration](../protocol/design.json). The [run matrix](../protocol/run-matrix.csv) and [manifest](../protocol/run-matrix-manifest.json) enumerate every planned execution and checksum the effective design and cohort files. The matrix is a schedule, not evidence of completed experiments.

The choice of 1,650 evaluations, 20 replicates and 24 instances sets a substantial prospective workload: 31.68 million objective evaluations. This evaluation limit was chosen before comparative results; its operational feasibility remains to be checked in the limited integration pilot of phase 6. Any change after that pilot must be documented here with a date and a new manifest before confirmatory results are inspected. Results from altered conditions cannot be pooled as though the design were unchanged.

Known limits: the environmental input has no clear redistribution license; assigned zero tap-water impacts are not measurements; some POF nutrient zeros can represent values not examined; the nutrient source is observational composition embedded in POF, not an independently verified clinical table; fixed foods and occasions allow quantity changes but no substitutions; and the balanced 24-day sample has no population prevalence interpretation. These limits are retained in the manuscript.
